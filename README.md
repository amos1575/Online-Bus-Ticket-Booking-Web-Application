1. **Project name**                                                                                                                                                          
**Online Bus Ticket Booking System** is a web application where passengers search trips, pick seats and get an e-ticket, and bus company admins manage routes, buses and schedules.

2. **Problem statement**                                                                                                                                                    
Travelers who buy tickets at the station often arrive to find the bus full, with no way to check seats beforehand.                                                          
• Wasted trips: passengers cannot see seat availability before leaving home.                                                                                               
• No seat choice: seats are assigned at the counter, so passengers cannot choose or preview them.                                                                             
• Queues and paper tickets: peak hours mean long lines, and paper tickets can be lost or sold twice.                                                                   
• Manual operations: companies track routes, schedules and sales by hand, which makes reports slow and error-prone.                                                         
3. **Objective**                                                                                                                                                            
Develop a web-based system that lets travelers book bus seats online and lets bus companies manage trips from one admin panel.                                             
Specific objectives:                                                                                                                                                       
.  Let passengers search trips by origin, destination and date, with live seat availability.                                                                                 
.  Let passengers choose seats on a seat map and book online.                                                                                                            
.  Issue an e-ticket with a unique booking code that can be downloaded or shown on a phone.                                                                               
.  Give admins an admin panel to manage routes, buses and schedules and to view bookings and sales reports.                                                               
.  Prevent the same seat from being booked twice for the same trip.

## 4. Scope of the Project

The system will focus on online ticket booking, seat selection, trip management, and ticket generation. It will provide passengers and administrators with a simple platform to manage bus transportation services efficiently.

## 5. Expected Benefits
. Saves passengers time by reducing queues.

. Makes seat availability easy to check.

. Reduces double booking.

. Improves bus company record management.

## 6. User Roles
- **Passenger**
- **Operator** 
- **Admin** 

## 7. Technologies Used
- ASP.NET Core Razor Pages (.NET 10).
- Entity Framework Core.
- ASP.NET Core Identity.
- SQL Server (managed with SSMS).
